Help and account control

Support

Contact

Open a request in the Bouncr website issue tracker. Response times are not guaranteed. Never post TikTok credentials, access tokens, private addresses, or other confidential information in an issue.

Disconnect TikTok

Open the Bouncr Creator Dashboard and choose Disconnect. The app asks TikTok to revoke the active token and then removes locally stored tokens.

Delete schedule data

Queued jobs can be cancelled. Finished, failed, and cancelled jobs can be deleted from the Queue section. For any production-server copy or backup, contact the privacy address shown in the Privacy Policy.

Failed posts

Keep the displayed TikTok log_id when available. Check that the account still grants the required scope, the video meets current limits, and the scheduler remained running at the selected time.
