Creator publishing workspace

Original simulations, reviewed and ready to publish.

Bouncr lets creators generate ball-simulation videos, preview the final media, choose TikTok publishing settings, and schedule an approved transfer from one focused dashboard.

Create

Build and record original physics-based simulations in the Bouncr desktop application.

Review

Preview the exact video, edit the caption, and actively choose privacy and interaction settings.

Schedule

Give explicit consent and select when the approved media should be securely transferred to TikTok.

Publishing workflow

The creator remains in control

  1. Connect your account.
    OAuth authorization is handled by TikTok. Bouncr never asks for a TikTok password.
  2. Inspect the final video.
    The dashboard shows a video preview and validates TikTok's current technical limits.
  3. Choose every setting.
    Privacy has no preset value. Comments, Duet, and Stitch start disabled until the creator turns them on.
  4. Consent and schedule.
    No media is transferred before the creator confirms the exact video, metadata, and time.
  5. Track processing.
    The dashboard displays upload progress and retrieves the publishing result from TikTok.
