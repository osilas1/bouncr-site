Publishing workflow

The creator remains in control

Bouncr does not send a video merely because it exists in a folder. Every scheduled transfer starts with a connected account, a reviewed file, explicit choices, and consent.

  1. Connect your account

    Authorization takes place on TikTok. Bouncr never asks for or stores a TikTok password.

  2. Inspect the final video

    The dashboard presents the exact media and checks format, codec, size, resolution, frame rate, and duration.

  3. Make every publishing choice

    Privacy has no preset selection. Interaction and disclosure settings stay under the creator's control.

  4. Confirm and schedule

    The creator confirms the selected video, metadata, music declaration, and scheduled time before anything is transferred.

  5. Track processing

    The local scheduler starts the requested transfer and retrieves the processing result for the dashboard.

Safety and transparency

Designed for deliberate, authentic publishing

No password collection

Account authorization uses TikTok's consent screen and can be revoked by the creator.

No hidden privacy default

The creator must actively select one of the privacy options currently available for their account.

No unattended content selection

Only the exact media and metadata approved in the dashboard are placed into the local queue.
